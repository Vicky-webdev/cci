Driving India's cultural economy

The CCI envisions an India where artisan communities are valued, respected, and empowered to sustain their heritage for contemporary relevance.


Support
Enabling intergenerational skill transfer, improving artisan livelihoods, and introducing appropriate tools and technology to help crafts scale up and remain viable.
Promote
Expanding market access and visibility for artisans through exhibitions, craft bazaars, design collaborations and international platforms that connect crafts to wider audiences.
Sustain
Reviving traditional crafts, supporting artisan resilience during crises, and strengthening the craft economy through better data, documentation, and policy integration.
